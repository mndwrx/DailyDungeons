⚠️ Quest Log: The Kitchen Dungeon

Adventurer, your procrastination has breached the seals of the Porcelain Domain. By abandoning the organic residue of your rations to the damp, room-temperature depths of the Basin, you have accidentally initiated a dark ritual. The local micro-fauna have feasted upon the fat and protein slurry, multiplying by the millions. As the oxygen in the stagnant water depleted, anaerobic decomposition took hold. The microscopic horrors began excreting volatile sulfur compounds and putrid fatty acids, weaving a miasma of rotten eggs and rancid dairy. This foul chemical reaction is no longer just a stench—the sheer density of the bacterial biomatter is coagulating. It is gaining sentience, feeding on the stuck-on gravy, and physically rising from the drain. Prepare your sponge and unsheathe your dish soap. The Dish Slime has spawned!
